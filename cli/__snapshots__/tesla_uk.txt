📜 Профіль
Bio: Inventor, electrical engineer, mechanical engineer, and futurist who is best known for his contributions to the design of the modern alternating current (AC) electricity supply system.
Quote: "The present is theirs; the future, for which I really worked, is mine."

🎨 Продукти та Мистецтво
 - Alternating Current (AC) System (service)
   Multi-phase power distribution system that enabled electrification of the modern world.
 - Tesla Coil (physical)
   An electrical resonant transformer circuit used to produce high-voltage, low-current, high frequency alternating-current electricity.
   Price: 33 WILLNI
 - Wardenclyffe Tower (Wireless Energy) (physical)
   An early experimental wireless transmission station designed for wireless world-wide telephony, broadcasting, and proof-of-concept for wireless power transmission.
   Price: 333 WILLNI
 - Induction Motor (physical)
   An AC motor where the electric current in the rotor needed to produce torque is obtained by electromagnetic induction from the magnetic field of the stator winding.

⏳ Хронологія
 [1856-07-10] Born in Smiljan, Austrian Empire (modern-day Croatia).
 [1875] Enrolled at Austrian Polytechnic in Graz on a Military Frontier scholarship.
 [1881] Moved to Budapest to work under Tivadar Puskás at a telegraph company, the Budapest Telephone Exchange.
 [1882] Began working for the Continental Edison Company in France, designing and making improvements to electrical equipment.
 [1884-06] Arrived in the USA and started working for Thomas Edison.
 [1887] Tesla Electric Light & Manufacturing was formed. Tested the first brushless alternating current induction motor.
 [1888-05] First presentation of the induction motor and multi-phase AC system to the AIEE.
 [1893] Chicago World's Fair (World's Columbian Exposition) lit by Tesla's AC system.
 [1899] Colorado Springs experiments: proof of wireless energy transmission through Earth and atmosphere.
 [1901] Construction of Wardenclyffe Tower began.
 [1943-01-07] Departed from the physical world at the New Yorker Hotel.
