📜 Профіль
Bio: Philosopher, poet, teacher, and composer. The 'Ukrainian Socrates' who spent most of his life as a wandering philosopher.
Quote: "The world tried to catch me, but didn't succeed."

🎨 Продукти та Мистецтво
 - The Garden of Divine Songs (art)
   A collection of 30 poems characterized by their depth, simplicity, and concern for the moral perfection of humanity.
 - Fables of Kharkiv (art)
   Collection of 30 philosophical fables, each ending with a moral called 'force'.
 - Narcissus. Know Thyself (digital)
   A philosophical treatise on the importance of self-knowledge and finding one's true nature.

⏳ Хронологія
 [1722-12-03] Born in Chornukhy, Lubny Regiment, Cossack Hetmanate.
 [1734] Enrolled at the Kyiv-Mohyla Academy, where he studied for almost 20 years with interruptions.
 [1741-11] Assigned as a singer to the Imperial Court Chapel in St. Petersburg.
 [1750-1753] Traveled abroad to Central/Western Europe, visiting Tokaj, Vienna, Presburg, Halle, and other cities.
 [1759] Invited to teach poetics at the Kharkiv Collegium.
 [1766] Wrote the treatise 'Entrance into Christian Goodness' for students of the Kharkiv Collegium.
 [1769] Began his life as a wandering philosopher after being dismissed from the Collegium for his unconventional views.
 [1794-11-09] Departed from the physical world in Pan-Ivanivka. Legend says he dug his own grave the day before.
